Two kinds of student, one approach

If maths feels like a wall

We find the exact point where things stopped making sense and rebuild from there, slowly, until the method feels like yours.

  • Gaps from earlier years filled properly
  • Exam technique and past papers
  • Calm, patient, no judgement

If school maths is too easy

We go past the syllabus into proof, problem solving and the kind of questions that appear in olympiads and admissions tests.

  • UKMT challenges and olympiad problems
  • MAT, TMUA and STEP preparation
  • A first taste of university maths
