# Where the name Cathy comes from

- Cathy is named after Chris's favourite maths teacher at school. (Chris told me, Oct 2026.)
- The idea: Harvey is the AI agent for law, and Cathy is the AI agent for maths. She has a person's name, not a product name.
- Why it matters: brand work (logos, tone, intros to parents) should feel like a person and a teacher, not abstract tech. Chris turned down a first set of geometric logo marks (Venn, sparks, a graph-paper C) for that reason.
